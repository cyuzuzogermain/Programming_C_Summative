The Internet of Things (IoT) connects everyday objects to the internet.
IoT devices collect data through sensors and share it over networks.
Smart homes use IoT thermostats, lights and door locks.
Security remains one of the biggest challenges in IoT deployments.

Many IoT systems rely on lightweight protocols such as MQTT and CoAP.
Edge computing reduces latency by processing iot data close to its source.
Agriculture benefits from soil moisture sensors and automated irrigation.
Healthcare uses wearable devices to monitor patients remotely.
An IoT gateway bridges local devices and cloud platforms; every IoT node reports to it.
Battery life and power consumption are key design constraints.
